**FicFairy** - download your AO3 bookmarks and collections, keep them up to date, and browse them in a web page.

A fork of [nianeyna/ao3downloader](https://github.com/nianeyna/ao3downloader) that swaps the console menu for a web app and a small local helper.

## Start here

- **[[Getting started]]** - download the app for Windows, Mac or Linux and run your first scan.
- **[[Runs]]** - which button to press, and what each one does.
- **[[Your library]]** - what gets saved where, and how files are named.
- **[[Troubleshooting]]** - rate limits, warnings, and what to do when something breaks.

Found a bug? Use the **Feedback** tab on the right edge of the app, or [open an issue](https://github.com/alexandria-p/ficfairy/issues). Feedback is posted publicly there; an attached run log has your AO3 username and password, IP addresses, emails, file paths and time zone taken out first.
