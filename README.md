# ao3downloader

Keep your own copy of your AO3 bookmarks, collections and favourite fics, and keep it up to date.
It runs in your browser and needs no command line.

## Download

| Windows | Mac (Apple silicon) | Mac (Intel) | Linux |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ao3downloader-windows.zip](https://github.com/alexandria-p/ao3downloader/releases/latest/download/ao3downloader-windows.zip) | [ao3downloader-macos-apple-silicon.zip](https://github.com/alexandria-p/ao3downloader/releases/latest/download/ao3downloader-macos-apple-silicon.zip) | [ao3downloader-macos-intel.zip](https://github.com/alexandria-p/ao3downloader/releases/latest/download/ao3downloader-macos-intel.zip) | [ao3downloader-linux-x86_64.zip](https://github.com/alexandria-p/ao3downloader/releases/latest/download/ao3downloader-linux-x86_64.zip) |

Unzip it, then start it:

- **Windows:** double-click `ao3downloader.exe`.
- **Mac:** double-click `Start ao3downloader.command`.
- **Linux:** run `Start ao3downloader.sh`.

The app opens in your browser; use Chrome or Edge. It isn't signed, so the first start asks you
to confirm: on Windows choose **More info > Run anyway**, and on a Mac go to **System Settings >
Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**. After that it updates itself with **Update now**.

Every version is on the [releases page](https://github.com/alexandria-p/ao3downloader/releases/latest).

## What it does

- **Your bookmarks, collections, or any fic by link or work number.** Download them as HTML, EPUB,
  PDF, MOBI or AZW3.
- **Logging in is optional.** Log in to include private bookmarks and fics restricted to AO3 users,
  or skip it to fetch only what anyone can see.
- **An index of everything you've saved**, so a **Quick Scan** fetches only what has changed since
  your last run instead of your whole library again.
- **Import a library you already have.** Point it at fics you downloaded before, and it indexes
  them, sorts out duplicates and dates, and keeps them up to date from then on.
- **Your files, where you want them:** a folder on your computer, or your Dropbox.
- **Browse and search your library**, with filters by tag, fandom and date, and your own custom
  collections.
