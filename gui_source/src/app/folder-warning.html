Before you choose a folder

A downloaded file is paired with its AO3 work by the work ID at the start of its file name, followed by a space, _, . or -. Anything after that is free.

✓ 34816549 No Paths Are Bound - Cal.html
× No Paths Are Bound 34816549.html — the number is not first
× 34816549No Paths Are Bound.html — nothing separates the number from the title

Files named any other way still load, and nothing will look broken - but they cannot be matched to your index, so every title opens on AO3 instead of your own copy. The line under the listing tells you how many works were matched, which is where you would notice.

The folder you choose becomes your library. If it does not already have them, it gets five folders of its own - indexing, collections, images, runs and works - and downloaded works live in works.
